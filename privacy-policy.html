Privacy Policy — KA

Updated: October 1, 2026

1. The Basics

KA (including the KA FAMILY, KA HOME, KA LIFE, KA Neuro, and KA Book sections) does not store the content of your profiles on a server. Everything you enter — profiles, names, birth dates, vaccination records, notes, moods, photos — is stored only locally, on your device. We have no access to this data, we never see it, and we cannot pass it on to anyone else, because we never physically receive it. The one exception is signing in (via Apple or Google): this uses the Supabase authentication service, which stores only the fact that you signed in (an account identifier and session) — not the content of your profiles or records.

2. What Data the App Collects

Creating a profile saves a name and the icon you picked for it. Inside KA FAMILY you can additionally enter a mom's/dad's/child's name and birth date (used for things like numerology calculations), vaccination records, notes, mood entries, feeding logs, and other records. The app uses the camera — for room photos in KA HOME, for fun mask photos in KA FAMILY's kids section, and to track your child's hand movements during KA Neuro finger exercises — as well as motion sensors/compass in KA HOME (to detect the room's orientation). All photos and sensor data are processed and saved locally on the device only; the app may also save finished images to your photo library. All of this is entered and controlled by the parent themselves.

3. Sharing Data Between Family Devices

The "Invite by code" feature (Baby → Access) currently runs in demo mode and does not send real data to any server. Once syncing between family devices becomes fully functional, this policy will be updated to describe exactly how and where data will be temporarily processed while it moves between devices.

4. Technical Data

The app keeps a few technical counters on the device (for example, message usage in demo mode) so features work correctly. This data never leaves the device.

5. Children and Health Data

KA is an app for parents. Some features involve a child's data, including vaccination records. This data is entered and fully controlled by the parent and stored only on their device — there is no separate account for the child.

6. Your Rights

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time, via Settings → Management → Delete Account. Because data is stored locally, deletion is immediate and irreversible.

7. Changes to This Policy

We'll update this policy if the app starts sending data to a server for real (for example, cloud sync). The date of the last update appears at the top of this document.

8. Contact

For any questions about how your data is handled, write to support@ka-app.ru.
